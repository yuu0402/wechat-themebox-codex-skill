# Payment And Service Asset Coverage

Payment, wallet, red-packet, transfer, card, shopping, and service-entry resources are high-impact WeChat areas. Do not skip them in a full theme pack.

If an image-generation tool rejects words such as payment, transfer, wallet, or red packet, keep the exact target filename in the production inventory and use a neutral visual description for the prompt. Example: describe "small secure card with coin/star motif" or "gift envelope action icon" while still exporting the exact ThemeBox filename. Tool sensitivity is not a reason to omit the asset.

## Required Coverage For Full Packs

Cover all matching names in `asset-naming.md` and any target export inventory. At minimum, look for these filename patterns:

- `wepay`

## Drawing Rules

- Keep payment/service icons original and generic; do not copy official WeChat Pay brand marks or merchant app logos.
- Use the original theme character's motifs, palette, and gestures to distinguish the icon.
- Keep the function clear: card, coin, envelope, transfer arrow, receipt, keyboard delete, loading state, shopping/service tile, or wallet compartment.
- Use transparent canvas for icon resources. Service-grid icons may have an internal illustrated object or small sticker shape, but no full rectangular background unless the target slot is a tile/surface.
- Export light/dark variants when listed and keep dimensions aligned.
- Do not replace this category with text labels. No text or watermark inside the icon.
